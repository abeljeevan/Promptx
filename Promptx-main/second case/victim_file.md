# THE SILENT WITNESS — Victim File

**Case ID:** SW-01
**Victim:** Dr. Meena Sen, 42, Project Director at Larkridge Observatory
**Cause of death:** Blunt-force trauma in the East Wing observation/service corridor

Meena was a principled radio-astronomy and signal-analysis researcher. She was investigating Project Door, an anomalous recurring signal, when she discovered missing archive sections, irregular timestamps, and unauthorized changes to research records. Her notes include: “Strange pattern. Repeats every 217 sec? Not natural. Check again.” and “The universe doesn't lie. People do.”

Her last confirmed activity was preparing material about the altered dataset. The central investigation is both who killed Meena and what she discovered that someone needed to disappear.
